# Create a quarterly revenue workbook

Create `evals/new-workbook-creation-control/output.xlsx` with one worksheet
named `Summary`. Put the headers Quarter, Revenue, Margin, and Gross profit in
`A1:D1`; put Q1 through Q4 in rows 2 through 5 with revenue values of 100000,
120000, 135000, and 150000 and margins of 40%, 42%, 43%, and 45%; and put a
Gross profit formula in column D for every quarter. Use readable, bold headers
plus US dollar and percentage number formats. Put `Scenario` in `A6` and
`Base` in `B6`, with a list on `B6` allowing Base, Upside, or Downside. Add at
`F2` a bar chart titled `Quarterly revenue` using the quarterly revenue data.
Do not add other sheets or unrequested content.

## Deliverables

- `evals/new-workbook-creation-control/output.xlsx`
